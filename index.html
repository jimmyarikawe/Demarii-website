From cocoa beans and raw cashew nuts to sesame, soybeans, non-GMO grains, and specialty spices—we manage the entire value chain from farmgate aggregation to destination port delivery.

Origin Sourcing & Farmgate Aggregation

We establish direct procurement contracts with verified farming cooperatives across West Africa, East Africa, and Southeast Asia—guaranteeing authentic provenance, traceability, and fair value retention.

Certified Quality Inspection & Lab Assays

Every batch undergoes stringent multi-point laboratory analysis for moisture content, purity percentage, bean count, outturn (KOR), and phytosanitary clearance certified by independent inspectors (SGS, Bureau Veritas).

Structured Trade Finance & Incoterms

We execute trades under standardized Incoterms 2020 (FOB, CIF, CFR, DAP) supported by international Letters of Credit (L/C at Sight, CAD), escrow structures, and comprehensive marine cargo insurance.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Trade Questions

Essential details on contract execution, Incoterms, quality assays, and payment structures for buyers and trading desks.

Which Incoterms does Demarii support for international shipments?

We execute shipments under standard ICC Incoterms 2020: FOB (Free On Board origin port), CIF (Cost, Insurance & Freight to destination port), CFR (Cost & Freight), and DAP (Delivered At Place for regional overland trades).

How do you guarantee commodity specifications and purity?

All export allocations are sampled, graded, and certified prior to container stuffing by accredited first-class independent survey companies (SGS, Bureau Veritas, or Cotecna). Certificates of Quality, Weight, Moisture, and Fumigation are included in the original shipping document package.

What payment structures and trade finance terms do you accept?

We work with 100% Irrevocable, Confirmed Letter of Credit at Sight (L/C at Sight) issued by a top-50 prime international bank, Cash Against Documents (CAD), and structured advance deposit arrangements for established long-term contract partners.

What is Demarii’s Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)?

Our standard containerized MOQ is 1 x 20ft FCL (approx. 14 to 19 MT depending on commodity density) or 1 x 40ft FCL. We also structure multi-thousand metric ton annual recurring supply contracts with staggered monthly container or breakbulk vessel shipments.

How are export documentation and customs compliance handled?

Demarii prepares the complete regulatory document dossier: Clean on Board Ocean Bill of Lading, Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Certificate of Origin (including EUR.1 / Form A where applicable), Phytosanitary Certificate, and Marine Cargo Insurance Policy.
